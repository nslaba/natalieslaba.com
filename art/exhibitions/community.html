Community Engagement

I've always had a longing to bring people together, and art has been my way of doing it: blowing bubbles out a gallery window onto the street below, staging a shadow show for strangers waiting at the train station right across the road, just to make them laugh; filling a room with people of every age and background to look at art, listen to music, and mingle.

From Uptown 7th Gallery, L'expo à Montréal avec Karim, and Silence in a Noisy World.
