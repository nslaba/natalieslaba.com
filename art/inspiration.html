Inspiration

Outdoors

I run and climb for the same reason I paint: to get somewhere quiet enough to hear myself. Most of what I understand about who I am, I've learned outside — on a trail before the day starts, partway up a rock face, waiting out a sunset on a summit with nothing left to do but look. The outdoors doesn't perform for me the way people sometimes do. It just is. Being alone with something that indifferent has a way of putting me back in my own body, and I keep going back to it to check in with myself and with where I stand in relation to everything around me.
