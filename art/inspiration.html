Inspiration

Outdoors

There's always a choice in the relationship you create with an environment, depending on the way you choose to move through it. In climbing, for example, you can choose to approach your surroundings with an intent to conquer. I don't like that. I like to climb to decipher; to learn what the rock is asking of me. Being humbled, and shown I still have so much to learn, is what I enjoy almost the most. And the more attunement and openness to learn I bring, the better I can read the rock.

Running is different. I let my thoughts run alongside me instead of leading. I feel bare, running under Squamish trees that have been here far longer than I have. That's the perspective I try to bring back into the studio.
